      PM2.5 readings from NEA via data.gov.sg. Photos from LTA traffic cameras, Changi Sailing Club and Windy.com webcams. Just for fun, not health advice.

      How to play

      A new round starts every 6 hours: 12am, 6am, 12pm and 6pm in Singapore. Everyone gets the same five photos, one from a live camera in each region: north, south, east, west and central.

      The photos are taken in the hour before the round starts. Guess that region's 1-hour PM2.5 from NEA for the same hour, in µg/m³. For the 6pm round that's the 5–6pm average. After each guess you'll see the real number, so use it for the next one.

      Each region is worth up to 200 points. You get full marks for an exact guess and less the further off you are. Being off by 2.5× in either direction scores zero.

      Your shareable result shows how close each region was (🟩 🟨 🟧 🟥) and which way you missed: ⬆️ too high, ⬇️ too low, 🎯 spot on.
